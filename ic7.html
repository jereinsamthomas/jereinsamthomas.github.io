The Virtual Brotherhood

More than a gaming clan.
We are a family.

Launched on October 10, 2020 (10-10-2020), IC7 Iconic Sevens quickly evolved from a group of enthusiastic gaming peers into Kerala's most tightly-knit eFootball clan community. We bring together tactical minds from every single district in Kerala under one virtual roof.

While eFootball squads and custom tactical formations are our primary battlefield, our community thrives on a deep brotherhood. Off the mobile pitch, our chat engine burns 24/7 with comprehensive real-time football metric breakdowns and massive cricket tournament watch-alongs.

We take pride in managing high-stakes competitive setups, hosting weekly coin custom rooms, and fostering young digital talents while standing united as a genuine internet family.

Discipline & Ethics

Clan Code of Conduct

We function like a family, but we maintain high competitive standards. Compliance with our core values is mandatory.

01

Absolute Respect

Treat every clan mate like blood. Toxicity, abusive verbal spams, or discrimination regarding football/cricket allegiances will never be tolerated.

02

Fair Play & Integrity

Any use of network manipulation, matchmaking exploits, or cheating in coin tours results in an instant, unappealable global clan ban.

!

Rules & Consequences

Failure to adhere to group codes will result in structural consequences: warnings, league point deductions, tournament suspensions, or permanent blacklist expulsion.

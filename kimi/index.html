2014 – 2018

Early Karting & WSK Titles

Dominant beginnings in Italian Easykart and 60 Mini ROK, clinching multiple international WSK trophies with exceptional racecraft.

OK-Junior International Debut

Graduated to OK-Junior, taking the WSK Euro Series title and finishing vice-champion in the FIA Karting European Championship.

2020 – 2021

Back-to-Back European Champion

Secured back-to-back FIA Karting European Championship titles (OK senior) with Kart Republic, hailed as a generational talent.

2022

Italian & ADAC Formula 4 Double

First single-seater campaign with Prema Racing: captured both the Italian F4 (13 wins) and ADAC F4 titles in record-breaking fashion.

2023

Formula Regional European Champion

Crowned Formula Regional Middle East champion and FRECA champion with 5 victories, sealing progression to Formula 2.

2024

FIA Formula 2 Winner & F1 Testing

Claimed stunning Formula 2 victories at Silverstone and the Hungaroring; completed comprehensive private F1 tests with Mercedes-AMG.
